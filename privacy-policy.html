Privacy Policy

This page explains how the Runique demonstration website is intended to handle information visitors enter into its forms. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Information you may provide

Reservation, contact, event inquiry, and newsletter forms may ask for details such as your name, email address, phone number, reservation preferences, or message.

How information is used

In a live service, these details would be used to respond to questions, manage reservation requests, coordinate events, or send newsletter updates where you have opted in.

Demo-site limitation

This is a front-end demonstration. The newsletter confirmation page does not send or store email addresses on a server. Other forms may not submit data to a real restaurant system. Do not enter sensitive personal information.

Data security and retention

A production website should explain its actual storage, access controls, retention period, service providers, and applicable data-protection rights. Those details must be completed to match the real implementation before this policy is used publicly.

For questions about this demo policy, use the Contact page.
