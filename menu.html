Kinilaw na Tangigue
₱450

Fresh line-caught Spanish Mackerel cured in spiced coconut vinegar, red onions, ginger, and bird's eye chilies. A refreshing, acidic start to awaken the palate.

Lumpiang Sariwa
₱320

A delicate crepe filled with hearts of palm, shrimp, and fresh vegetables, drizzled with a sweet and savory garlic-peanut sauce.

Crispy Ukoy
₱280

Crispy fritters made from small shrimp, sweet potato, and bean sprouts. Served with a house-spiced vinegar dipping sauce.

Beef Kaldereta
₱950

Our signature take on a Filipino classic. We take premium bone-in short ribs and slow-braise them for 12 hours over live charcoal. The rich tomato stew is thickened naturally with artisanal liver pâté.

Kare-Kare
₱850

A rich and savory stew made with tender oxtail, crushed peanuts, toasted rice, and native vegetables. Served alongside our own house-fermented shrimp paste (bagoong).

Lechon Kawali
₱750

Pork belly boiled with aromatics then deep-fried until blistered and crispy. Served with traditional liver sauce and spiced vinegar.

Inihaw na Liempo
₱650

Thick-cut pork belly marinated in soy sauce, calamansi, and garlic, then grilled directly over wood fire. Glazed with a sweet banana ketchup reduction.

Ube Cheese Tart
₱350

A buttery pastry shell filled with homemade purple yam (ube) halaya, topped with a rich Basque-style burnt cheese layer.

Suman at Mangga
₱290

Steamed glutinous rice wrapped in banana leaves, served with perfectly ripe Guimaras mangoes and a sweet coconut cream reduction.

Calamansi Margarita
₱400

A Filipino twist on the classic. 100% Agave silver tequila shaken with freshly squeezed local calamansi, orange liqueur, and agave nectar. Served with a chili-salt rim.

Lambanog Sour
₱380

Traditional Philippine coconut spirit (Lambanog) crafted into a silky sour with fresh lemon juice, egg white, and a dash of Angostura bitters.

Don Papa Old Fashioned
₱450

Premium aged Philippine rum from Negros, stirred down with rich muscovado sugar syrup and artisanal bitters. Garnished with a torched orange peel.
