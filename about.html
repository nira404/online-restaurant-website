Our Story

Crafting moments,
not just meals.

Runique was born from a simple idea in 2011: to elevate the heirloom recipes we grew up eating. By stripping away the noise and focusing on live-fire cooking techniques, we allow the natural, robust flavors of local Filipino ingredients to speak for themselves. We don't just serve food; we preserve heritage.

Our Purveyors

Rooted in the Soil

Our menu changes with the seasons, dictated entirely by what the land provides. We work directly with independent farmers in Batangas for our heirloom vegetables and partner with line-fishermen in the surrounding provinces to ensure sustainable, ethical sourcing.

Even the vessels your food is served on carry a story—each ceramic plate is hand-thrown and kiln-fired by local artisans, ensuring that every element of your dining experience is inherently, authentically Filipino.
